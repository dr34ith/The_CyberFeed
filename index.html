CyberFeed

Your personal cybersecurity intelligence dashboard. A GitHub Action refreshes the article snapshot from all 18 sources every 5 minutes; this page polls it in the background every 2 minutes and offers new stories as they land, with a live fallback fetch when the snapshot is unavailable.

Data & privacy

  • Article thumbnails may load directly from the originating publisher.
  • Books you upload are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB) and are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Bookmarks, read status, and tags are stored locally on this device.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • / or Ctrl+K focus search
  • j / k navigate articles
  • b bookmark, o open, t theme
  • Reader shortcuts are listed inside the Books reader.
